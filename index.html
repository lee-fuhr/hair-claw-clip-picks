How we choose our picks

We do not claim to have tested every clip ourselves. Instead, we rely on a combination of long-term owner feedback from retail reviews, hair-community forums, and published evaluations by trusted beauty editors. We track what holds up over months of use, things like spring strength, material quality, and resistance to cracking or slipping, and we flag the models that consistently disappoint. Our research also digs into the common failure points: cheap plastic that snaps, springs that lose tension after a few weeks, and clips that are too small or too shallow for thick hair. We weigh these durability factors alongside ergonomics and overall value, so our picks reflect what real owners actually recommend to friends.
