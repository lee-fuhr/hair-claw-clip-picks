Claw clips have made a major comeback, and for good reason: they’re gentle on hair, quick to use, and surprisingly versatile. Whether you have fine, medium, or very thick hair, the right claw clip can hold a twist, a bun, or a half-up style all day without slipping or pulling. When choosing a claw clip, pay attention to the spring tension, the teeth length and spacing, and the material. Larger clips with stronger springs work best for thick or heavy hair, while softer springs suit finer textures. Acetate and high-quality plastic resist cracking better than cheap options.
